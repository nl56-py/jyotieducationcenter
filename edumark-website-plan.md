**Badge:** `SERVICES`


#### 1. 🧭 Services Intro

Overview of EduMark's student support model. Mention 500+ colleges/universities, multi-destination support, and end-to-end guidance.


#### 2. 🎓 Career Counselling

Personalized counseling to help students identify the right country, course, and university based on their academic background, goals, and budget.


#### 3. 📄 Admission Guidance

Full application support: university selection, entry requirement checks, SOP writing, CV preparation, reference letters, and application submission.


#### 4. 🛂 Visa Assistance

End-to-end visa documentation support, financial document preparation, interview preparation, and visa filing for all supported study destinations.


#### ✦. ⚡ Tricky CTA Block (Mid-Page)

"Last Chance Alert — Limited Seats for October & September 2026 Intakes! Book your free counseling now." Strong urgency message with a DM/call-to-action button. This is placed between Visa Assistance and the next section.


#### 5. 🏫 Information on Institutions

Guidance on comparing universities, understanding accreditations (NIRF, NBA, AICTE, QS Rankings, THE Rankings), and making an informed final choice.


#### 6. ✈ Travel Assistance

Support with travel planning, flight booking guidance, and airport procedures for students flying to their chosen destination.


#### 7. 🏠 Accommodation Assistance

Help finding suitable student housing, hostels, or university accommodation arrangements before departure.


#### 8. 🧳 Pre-Departure Briefings

Comprehensive pre-departure session covering: destination country culture, weather, academic norms, financial management tips, emergency contacts, and what to pack.
